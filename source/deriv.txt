@deriv w2 | Incremental mean update

S: $$\mu_k = \frac{1}{k}\sum_{j=1}^{k} x_j$$
W: Definition of the mean of $k$ samples.

W: Pull the newest sample out of the sum.
S: $$\mu_k = \frac{1}{k}\left(x_k + (k-1)\mu_{k-1}\right)$$
W: The remaining sum is $(k-1)$ times the previous mean.

W: Expand and rearrange. Memory needed: only the old mean and a counter.
S: $$Q(A) \leftarrow Q(A) + \frac{1}{N(A)}\left(R - Q(A)\right) \qquad V(S_t) \leftarrow V(S_t) + \alpha\left(G_t - V(S_t)\right)$$
